Our Story

Crafted with Precision, Defined by Quality

Guangzhou Kadiluo Packaging Products Co., Ltd. was founded in 2008, covering a factory area of over 14,000 square meters. We specialize in perfume bottle packaging materials for domestic and international markets, integrating design, R&D, production, and sales into one comprehensive manufacturer.

The company is equipped with imported machinery for hot silver stamping, silk screen printing, drying, and spray coating, along with integrated and automated production lines. With years of production and sales experience, our company is well-established, featuring complete process workshops for mold making, frosting, hot gold stamping, and quality inspection. Our workforce exceeds 100 employees, with a daily output of over 200,000 units. We specialize in high-end glass bottles, perfume bottles, and aromatherapy bottles, offering custom mold design with top-tier product quality. We maintain ample stock for immediate supply.

We provide high-quality products and satisfactory services to domestic and international customers, earning the trust and consistent recognition of our clients. Welcome friends from all walks of life to visit, guide, and discuss business cooperation.
